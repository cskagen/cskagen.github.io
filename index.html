Christian Skagen is a Norwegian visual artist working with systematic, meditative ink drawing. The drawings are constructed as linear fields through layered parallel line systems, accumulating measurable distance — often reaching kilometers of total line length — built from repeated signals (individual lines). The archive currently spans numbered drawings from tegning nr 001 to tegning nr 877.
